## 1. Identity & Positioning

- **Core Strategic Positioning**:
  > *"SFPE Australia is the window between Australian practice and international fire safety engineering research and practice."*
- **Relationship with Society of Fire Safety (SFS / Engineers Australia)**:
  - SFPE Australia does not compete with or mirror SFS.
  - Domestic matters: collaborative partnerships with domestic institutions (SFS, AIDGC, Universities).
  - International knowledge transfer: led and owned by SFPE Australia through the global network of The Society of Fire Protection Engineers.
- **Key Differentiator**:
  - Many members are internationally trained, holding postgraduate credentials and cross-border project experience that extends beyond traditional domestic pathways.
  - SFS is operating within Engineers Australia institutional constraints; SFPE Australia operates as an agile, technically focused chapter.

## 2. Technical Webinar Program (6 in 12 Months)

- **Target**: 6 high-impact technical webinars delivered over the next 12 months (4 confirmed delivered represents an outstanding outcome).
- **The Discussant Model**:
  - Global presenter (SFPE handbook author, international researcher, or sister chapter specialist).
  - Australian discussant (local senior engineer or academic who "Australianizes" the content for NCC/BCA compliance).
- **Confirmed Partner Pipeline**:
  1. **Society of Fire Safety (SFS)**: Co-badged domestic/international seminar.
  2. **SFPE New Zealand Chapter**: Trans-Tasman comparative verification (C/VM2 vs NCC).
  3. **SFPE UK Chapter**: Post-October UK conference debrief and structural fire design.
  4. **AIDGC (Sean Page, President)**: Crossover between fire engineering and dangerous goods storage.
- **Additional Focus Areas**:
  - Authors of the new *SFPE Handbook of Fire Protection Engineering* (6th Edition).
  - Fire Safety Research Institute (UL FSRI) and NFPA Fire Protection Research Foundation webinars.
  - Critical infrastructure resilience: Battery Energy Storage Systems (BESS) and high-density data centers.
  - Structural fire resistance, mass timber, and heritage building conservation.
- **CPD Recognition**:
  - Self-reported continuing professional development (CPD) logged under Engineers Australia Type II & Type III categories (zero accreditation barriers).
